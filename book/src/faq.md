# Troubleshooting and FAQ

**Solrac says my key is on three machines.** Free one from the list it shows, or choose
**Deactivate this machine** in Preferences → Licence on the machine you no longer use.

**It says it can't reach the licence service.** Solrac keeps working on its saved
certificate for 30 days; connect to the internet once and it renews.

**The chat doesn't answer.** It needs a licence seat on this machine or a signed-in account, and
tokens to pay with. Check Preferences → Account.

**It doesn't hear me.** Hold Space or Talk and speak for at least a moment. macOS must have
allowed Solrac to use the microphone (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone).
The voice models download once on first use, so the first try may fall back to online
transcription.

**cb2026 stopped moving.** **Game → Restart cb2026**. If you are playing on another
computer's engine, see [that chapter's troubleshooting](network-play.md#when-something-goes-wrong).

**Where are my games?** In **File → Games Library** (⌘L). Saved PGNs are in
`Documents/Solrac`.

**The board looks too busy or slow.** Lower the sliders in Preferences → Lights or choose a
plain Environment in Display. On a computer with a weak graphics card, choose the 2D board
(Preferences → Chess Set), or Quality Low (Preferences → Display); see
[On a slower computer](slow-computers.md).

**Solrac doesn't read the replies aloud.** On a computer with a weak graphics card, Automatic
reads only in the Rail's Voice mode. Choose **Always** under Preferences → Avatar → Voice →
Read replies aloud, or use the speaker button in the Rail.

**Something else.** Post it on the [issues page](https://github.com/cjus/solrac-chess/issues).
