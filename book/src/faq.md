# Troubleshooting and FAQ

**Solrac says my key is on three machines.** It lists them, with when each was last seen.
Choose **Deactivate** beside the one you no longer use, or **Use another key**. Another
machine can be freed twice in 30 days. On a machine you still have, choose **Deactivate this
machine** in **Preferences → Licence**. **Lost your key?** on the same screen has the website
email it again.

**It says it can't reach the licence service.** Solrac keeps working on its saved
certificate for 30 days; connect to the internet once and it renews. If it can't confirm the
licence at all, choose **Try again**.

**The chat doesn't answer.** It needs a licence seat on this machine or a signed-in account, and
tokens to pay with. Check Preferences → Account.

**It doesn't hear me.** Hold Space or the Talk button and speak for at least a moment (up to 30 seconds). macOS must have
allowed Solrac to use the microphone (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone).
The voice models download once on first use, so the first try may fall back to online
transcription.

**Solrac stopped moving.** If the status line says there is an engine error, choose **Restart Solrac**, the button under it in the panel: it starts the engine again and the game carries on. If you are playing on another computer's engine, see [that chapter's troubleshooting](network-play.md#when-something-goes-wrong).

**Where are my games?** In **File → Games Library** (⌘L). Saved PGNs are in the **Solrac**
folder in your Documents folder.

**The board looks too busy or slow.** Lower the sliders in Preferences → Lights or choose a
plain Environment in Display. On a computer with a weak graphics card, choose the 2D board
(Preferences → Chess Set), or Quality Low (Preferences → Display); see
[On a slower computer](slow-computers.md).

**Solrac doesn't read the replies aloud.** On a computer with a weak graphics card, Automatic
reads only in the Rail's Voice mode. Choose **Always** under Preferences → Avatar → Voice →
Read replies aloud, or use the speaker button in the Rail. Check that it isn't set to **Never**.

**Something else.** Post it on the [issues page](https://github.com/cjus/solrac-chess/issues).
