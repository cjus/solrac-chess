# Release notes

## 1.0.0

The first release: the 3D board and piece sets, play against cb2026 at any level with clocks,
review and analysis, Engine Thinking and Engine Mind, the chat with hands-free voice, the
games library with import and backups, and your licence and account.

Playing on an engine on another computer is new and needs a cb2026 built with its network
feature; see [that chapter](network-play.md).
