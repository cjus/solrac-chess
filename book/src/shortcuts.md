# Keyboard shortcuts

Every key Solrac answers to. Press **F1** in Solrac for the same list. Keys are written for a Mac, with the Windows and Linux key in brackets where it differs (Ctrl takes the place of ⌘). Each button in the panel also says what it does when you point at it.

## General

- **Tab**: Hide or show the whole interface: the panel, the menu bar and the chat strip. It works while you are typing in the chat, which keeps what you typed
- **Esc**: Close the topmost of New Game, Feedback, About, Preferences, Games and Engine Thinking. It also stops a line the chat is showing, puts away a move preview, takes the keys back from the move list, and in Engine Mind lets go of a selected move, then closes a window, then leaves the view
- **⌘, (Ctrl+,)**: Open Preferences
- **F1** (⌘? on a Mac): Open Help
- **⌘Q (Alt+F4)**: Quit Solrac

## File

- **⌘N (Ctrl+N)**: New Game
- **⌘L (Ctrl+L)**: Games Library

## View

- **⌘E (Ctrl+E)**: Engine Thinking
- **⌘↑ (Ctrl+↑)**: Engine Mind behind the board
- **⌘↓ (Ctrl+↓)**: Back to the board
- **⌘+ (Ctrl++)**: Larger Text
- **⌘− (Ctrl+−)**: Smaller Text

## Camera

These work only while the 3D camera is unlocked (the panel's **Lock** button), and not while the move list or a text box has the keys. On the 2D board use the panel's **Flip** button or **View → Flip Board**.

- **← → ↑ ↓**: Turn and tilt the camera, a step a press, continuously while held
- **F**: Flip the board
- **Z**: Zoom to fit
- **R**: Reset the camera view

## Board

- **L**: Show or hide the board's labels (not while you are typing)

## Voice

- **Space**: Hold to talk (when you are not typing)

## Move list

- **←**: Previous move (once a move in the list is clicked)
- **→**: Next move
- **Home**: First move
- **End**: Last move
- **↑ ↓**: Where a loaded game has variations: the other moves that could have been played instead of the move shown. ↓ goes down a variation and on past its end; ↑ backs out
